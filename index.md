#   Multi-Format Games League

The Multi-Game Games League is a cross-game competition designed to encourage participation in a variety of tabletop games.

The league uses...
- A Swiss-style "challenge" approach with "home" and "away" games
- ... and a common points structure across all registered games
- ... and a game-specific proficiency rating system
- ... and awards points for participation as well as winning games.

The objective of the league is to:
-    Encourage players to participate in a variety of games.
-    Encourage matches between players of different proficiency levels.
-    Reward participation, not just winning.
-    Encourage experienced players to play less experienced opponents.
-    Encourage players to learn new games and develop their skills.
-    Provide one overall league competition covering multiple games.

The guiding principle is:

    Playing, participating and taking on challenges should be worthwhile, regardless of the final result.
