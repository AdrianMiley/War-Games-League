#   Playing A Match

the League is intended to be a friendly and inclusive environment for all players, regardless of their skill level or experience.

Due to the potential number of players and the potential frequency of games (one per week) it is infeasible for every registered player tio play every other registered player. 
Consequently the League will operate on a **swiss-style challenge system** where any player issues a challenge to any other player that they want to play at some game.

##  Challenging Opponents

Challenges are issued by a Challenger and once accepted the Match is announced and registered with the League Organizer as a future Match. 

A Match consist of two games where the two players play "home" and "away".

In each Game the "away" player chooses the game to be played from the games registered by the "home" player.
In the reverse fixture the "home" and "away" players swap roles.

No player may challenge another player more than once in a season.

###  Multi-Player Games

Where a game allows for more than two players (i.e. most Board Games) then additional players can ask to join a match once it is announced. 
If they do and the return fixture is also a game that accommodates the same number of players then they must also participate in that match as well.

Joining an announced match does not count as issuing a challenge to either of the players that arranged the Match.
