# Player Registration

Every player must register the games they are willing and able or interested in playing. 
These will be the games that they will be able to play in the league and the list from which an opponent must select a game for an agreed fixture.

For each game, players must provide:
-    Game name.
-    Rules edition or version, where applicable.
-    Proficiency rating (1–5).

A player can optionally register a game that they *will not* play in the league

Players may also register additional games during the season.

Once a game has been selected for an agreed fixture, it cannot be removed from the player's registration until that fixture has been completed.

##  Player Proficiency Ratings

Proficiency ratings are used to assess a player's skill level in a particular game. The ratings are as follows:

| Level | Rating | Played       | Description                                    | 
|-------|--------|--------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Not Played   | 0 games      | Not played the game but interested in learning | 
| 2     | Beginner     | 1 to 3 games | Player has basic knowledge of the game         | 
| 3     | Intermediate | 3 to 10      | Player has good knowledge of the game          | 
| 4     | Advanced | 11 to 20     | Player has extensive knowledge of the game     | 
| 5     | Expert | 20+          | Player is highly skilled in the game           | 

For each game the player must provide a proficiency rating based on their experience and skill level. 
Ratings are self-assessed but players should be honest to ensure fair competition.
The league organiser may review ratings where there is a reasonable concern that they are inaccurate.

The initial proficiency Ratings are subsequently updated as games are played and players improve their skills in each game.
