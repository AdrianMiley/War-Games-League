#    Match Scoring

Every completed match contributes points to the league table.

The maximum normal score for an individual match is 12 points broken down as follows...

| Game Result       | Min Points | Max Points |
|:------------------|:-----------|:-----------|
| Participation     | 3          | 3          |
| Proficiency Bonus | 0          | 4          |
| Match Result      | 1          | 5          |

## Participation Points

Both players receive 3 points for completing a match, regardless of the result. 

This is simply to encourage players to participate in more matches and to reward them for doing so.

## Proficiency Bonus

To encourage more proficient players to play less proficient opponents (and vice versa), a Proficiency Bonus is awarded based on the relative difference between the players' proficiency ratings for that particular game.
**Both players are awarded this Proficiency Bonus** regardless of the result of the actual game.

The Proficiency Bonus is calculated by subtracting the higher proficiency rating from the lower proficiency rating to determine the Level Difference.

For example, if an Advanced player (Level 4) plays a Beginner (Level 2) then each player receives 2 additional Proficiency Bonus point i.e. level 4 minus Level 2 is a 2 Level Difference.

In a Multi-Player Match between more than two players then the Proficiency Bonus is calculated as the difference between the highest rated player and the lowest rated player and all players receive this Proficiency Bonus.
This is the reflect the fact that all players, not just the most advanced player, will be helping the lowest rated player to play the game.

##  Match Result

Traditionally, most League systems award points for winning a match and nothing for losing. 
However, this does not reflect the fact that some games are closer than others and a player who loses a close game should be rewarded for their effort rather than the winner getting all the spoils.

Consequently, to provide a wider range of results and to reward players for close games, the following points are awarded based on the outcome of the match.

| Outcome       | Points |
|:--------------|:-------|
| Decisive Win  | 5      |
| Close Win     | 4      |
| Draw          | 3      |
| Close Loss    | 2      |
| Decisive Loss | 1      |

### Determining a Decisive or Close result 

Many Table-Top Games use a points based system to balance a game either to determine how a player constructs their army or by accumulating "victory points" as the game progresses

A Close Game is defined as a game where the points difference between the winning player and the losing player is within a 10% range.

> Design Note: Calculating a percentage rather than explicit value range avoids having to deal with games that utilize higher points value.
> For example, "_Kings of War_" generally involves 2,300 points for a complete army whereas "_Lion Rampant_" uses 30 points to cost a complete army.
> Board Games have the same kind of disparity in scoring systems.
> This could be refined over time to tweak winning margins if we find that 10% difference is not always a "Close Win" in all games 

How each players points are calculated will generally be defined according to the rules being played such as accumulated victory point, objective scores, casualties or scenario outcomes.

The same thresholds will be applied consistently to all players playing that game and scenario.

##  Example

### Two Player Match

Player A (Advanced) plays Player B (Beginner) and scores a Decisive Win then the points awarded would be...

| Category      | Player A | Player B | Note                                                        |
|---------------|----------|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Participation | 3        | 3        |                                                             |
| Result        | 5        | 1        |                                                             |
| Proficiency   | 2        | 2        | Advanced (4) minus Beginner (2) equals 2 Proficiency Points |
|               |          |          |                                                             |
| Total         | 10       | 6        |                                                             |

Player A receives 10 points but Player B also receives 6 points despite losing the game decisively.

This is an important feature of the league: 

    challenging a stronger opponent should still be rewarding even if it's a slaughter

### Multi-Player Match

Four players play a game where Player A is Advanced, Players B and C are Intermediate and Player B is a Beginner and the platers score as dollows...

| Player | Proficiency  | Score |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| A      | Intermediate | 100   |
| B      | Expert       | 90    |
| C      | Beginner     | 70    |
| D      | Novice       | 40    |

| Category      | Player A | Player B | Player C | Player D | Note |
|---------------|----------|----------|----------|----------|-----|
| Participation | 3        | 3        | 3        | 3        | (1) |
| Result        | 4        | 2        | 1        | 1        | (2) & (3) |
| Proficiency   | 4        | 4        | 4        | 4        | (4) |
|               |          |          |          |          |     |
| Total         | 11       | 9        | 8        | 8        |     |

1. All players receive 3 points for participation.
2. Player A scored the most points so is the winner but only beat the next highest scorer by 10 points (10%) so it's a Close Win (4 points)
3. Player B receives 2 points for a Close Loss but Players C and Player D only receive 1 point as Decisive Losers relative to the winner.
4. Player B is the most proficient player and player D is least proficient so Proficiency Bonus is calculated a the difference between these two values.
